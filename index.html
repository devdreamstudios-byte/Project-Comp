01Briefing

What is Project Comp

Project Comp is a free, community-built take on Gorilla Tag. Same arm-powered movement you already know, rebuilt around ranked lobbies, custom maps and a leaderboard that actually tracks who is climbing.

01

Movement first

Arm-powered climbing, pushing and momentum that stays true to the game you already know how to play.

02

Ranked lobbies

Every registered player carries a Comp score, so lobbies fill with people near your level.

03

Custom maps

Community maps and game modes rotate through the servers instead of one static arena.

04

Free and community run

No store, no paywalls. Register your account and the builds unlock.
